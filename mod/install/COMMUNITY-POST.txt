**CupheadVR 1.02: Cuphead in VR as a pop-up storybook stage (free mod, OpenXR)**

Cuphead on a stage floating in front of you, in true native stereo: both eyes are drawn from the same game tick, with no alternate-eye rendering and no warping. The game's own parallax layers become real depth, so the hand-painted backgrounds sit far behind, Cuphead and the bosses stand out like cut-outs, and the foreground leans out toward you. Play it seated with a gamepad.

**What it does**
- Native stereo through OpenXR, with depth taken from the game's own parallax layers
- Smooth motion at your headset's refresh rate (72, 90, 120 Hz and up), at exactly the normal game speed
- Resolution set from your headset's pixel density and the stage size
- In-headset settings menu styled like the game's own cards. Hold both sticks to open it
- Settings for stage size and distance, layer depth, character pop, pop-out, sky distance, film grain, colour fringe, sharpening and resolution
- Installs with one bat file, and uninstalls the same way. No game files are changed

**What you need**
- The Steam version of Cuphead
- A PC VR headset with OpenXR: SteamVR set as your OpenXR runtime, or Virtual Desktop's VDXR
- A gamepad

**Please read**
- Tested in real headsets on two setups: Pimax Dream Air through SteamVR, and Meta Quest 3 through Virtual Desktop's VDXR. Other headsets and graphics cards should work but have not been tried. Reports are welcome.
- The overworld map stays flat on the stage, because its art has no depth layers.
- The mod is not signed, so Windows may warn you, and Smart App Control can block it. The README covers this.

**Install**
Unzip it, double click "Install CupheadVR.bat", and type 1. Start SteamVR or connect Virtual Desktop, then start Cuphead from Steam.
